Restaurant & Fire Pit

Nourish and gather, with the water left on.

Earth-based cooking at Raíz from morning to the long table, then the evening around the fire at Embér.

Raíz RestaurantEmbér Fire Pit
Raíz
Restaurant

Earth-based cooking, beside the waterfall.

Raíz means root, and the kitchen cooks that way. Wood fire, local salt, one dish done well, with a menu that follows the season and the village. The only music is the water moving over stone, held between thirty and forty decibels so a table can slow down.

On Fridays sixteen guests gather at the long table, the social centre of the resort.

Embér
Fire Pit

Evenings, around open flame.

When the light goes, Amazona gathers at Embér. A sunken fire pit ringed with cushions, set under the canopy and strung with soft light. Stories, sound baths in the dark and a last cup of tea before sleep.

Nothing is scheduled and nobody performs. You arrive alone and leave known.
