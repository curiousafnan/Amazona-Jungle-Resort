The Concept

A wellness sanctuary built into the living jungle.

Amazona is a functional masterwork of seven cascading levels in the Kaba-Kaba region of Bali, a hidden haven for those who want to slow down and live longer. Lago sits over untouched jungle, ĀYLA and Tierra Spa work low in the basalt, Raíz cooks to the sound of falling water, and Embér gathers everyone at dark.

Disconnect to reconnect.
