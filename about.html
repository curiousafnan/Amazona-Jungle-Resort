About Amazona Jungle Resort

A hidden haven in the Kaba-Kaba jungle.

A COCO Development Group collection of forty-one residences across seven cascading levels, built around recovery, nature and community.

Our Story

Disconnect to reconnect.

Amazona sits above untouched jungle in Cepaka, fifteen minutes inland from Canggu. It was designed around a single idea: recovery as a way of life, not a weekend. From the team behind Coco Lifestyle Resort, it brings the same thinking into the jungle, slower and quieter.

Good to Know

Frequently asked.

Where is Amazona?

In Kaba-Kaba, Cepaka, in the jungle about fifteen minutes inland from Canggu, Bali.

When does Amazona open?

Amazona opens in 2026. Early reservations are open now through the booking page.

Who is Amazona for?

Couples, solo travellers, families and remote workers who want to slow down, recover and stay a little longer.

What is included with a residence?

Every residence includes full access to Lago, ĀYLA, Prána, Aluna Garden and Embér, with Raíz and Tierra Spa on site. Treatments and meals are arranged during your stay.

Are longer stays cheaper?

Yes. Five nights or more unlock an automatic discount, rising to 20% off for stays of thirty nights.

Can I own a residence at Amazona?

Ownership and long-term residency enquiries are welcome. Speak with our team for current availability.

Who is behind Amazona?

Amazona is developed and managed by COCO Development Group, the team behind Coco Lifestyle Resort in Uluwatu.
