Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Amazona Jungle Resort (“Amazona”, “we”, “us”) is a COCO Development Group brand in Kaba-Kaba, Cepaka, Tabanan, Bali, Indonesia. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit amazonajungleresort.com, contact us or stay with us, why we use it, and the choices you have.

We handle personal data in line with Indonesia’s Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022). Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies to you, you also have the rights it gives you.

1. What we collect

  • Details you give us. Your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, stay dates, number of guests, the residence you are interested in, and anything you add in a message or request, such as arrival time, dietary needs or a Tierra Spa treatment.
  • Booking and payment details. Card payments are made through a secure payment link from our payment provider. We do not see or store your full card number. If you pay by bank transfer, we keep the transfer record.
  • Stay details. At check-in we may need to see and record your passport or ID, because accommodation providers in Indonesia must report guest details to the authorities.
  • Health information you choose to share. If you tell us about an injury, pregnancy or health condition before a spa treatment or wellness session, we use it only to keep you safe during that session.
  • Website data. The site remembers the currency you pick (USD, IDR or EUR) in your own browser. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, to keep the site secure and working. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies at present.

2. Services from other companies on this site

Some features are provided by other companies, which receive basic technical data such as your IP address when you use them:

  • Google Fonts loads the typefaces used on the site.
  • Google Maps shows our location on the Contact page.
  • WhatsApp carries any message you send us through our WhatsApp links.
  • Instagram opens if you follow the link to our profile.

Each of these companies handles that data under its own privacy policy.

3. How we use your data

  • To answer your questions and messages.
  • To arrange, confirm and manage your reservation and your stay.
  • To take payment and keep financial records.
  • To send you practical information before you arrive.
  • To let you know when reservations open, if you registered your interest.
  • To meet our legal duties, including guest registration, tax and accounting.
  • To keep the website and our systems secure.

We only send you offers or news if you have agreed to receive them, and you can stop them at any time.

4. Our legal reasons for using your data

We use your data because it is needed to provide the stay or service you asked for, because the law requires it, because you have given consent, or because we have a legitimate interest in running and protecting our business in a way that does not override your rights.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal data only where it is needed:

  • With companies that work for us, such as our payment provider, our customer relationship and messaging tools (HighLevel, which receives the forms on this site through Zapier), email and booking tools, website hosting and WhatsApp.
  • With COCO Development Group companies that help us operate the resort.
  • With government authorities when Indonesian law requires it.

We never sell your personal data.

6. Data stored outside Indonesia

Some of the companies we work with store data on servers outside Indonesia. When that happens, we use providers that protect personal data to the standard the law requires.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to answer you and follow up. We keep booking, payment and guest registration records for as long as Indonesian tax, accounting and registration rules require. After that, we delete or anonymise them.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data, where we no longer need it or are not required to keep it;
  • stop or limit how we use it, or withdraw consent you gave earlier.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm your identity first. If you are unhappy with how we handled your data, you can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

9. Keeping your data safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, and we limit access to the people who need it to do their work. No method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send card numbers or passport copies through chat or email.

10. Children

This website is not aimed at children. Reservations must be made by an adult aged 18 or over, who may include children’s names and ages in a booking.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add new services to the website. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
