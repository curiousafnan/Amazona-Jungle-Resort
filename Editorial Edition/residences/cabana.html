The residence

A residence, not a room.

One bedroom, an open living space and a private terrace with its own jacuzzi, finished in warm timber and stone. Morning light comes through the trees, and the whole resort is a short walk down the levels. Made for solo travellers, remote workers and couples who want everything they need in one considered space.
