The concept

A wellness sanctuary built into the living jungle.

Amazona is a functional masterwork of seven cascading levels in the Kaba-Kaba region of Bali, a hidden haven for those who want to slow down and live longer. Lago sits over untouched jungle, ĀYLA and Tierra Spa work low in the basalt, Raíz cooks to the sound of falling water, and Embér gathers everyone at dark.

Seven destinations

One language, one rhythm.

Seven destinations, each tuned to a different hour of the day, from first light at Prána to the last of the fire at Embér.

Prána

An open-air pavilion for movement, breath and stillness.

Lago

An infinity pool held over untouched jungle.

ĀYLA

Heat, cold and rest, worked low into the basalt.

Tierra Spa

Warm mud, slow hands and silence.

Aluna Garden

Daybeds and low lounges set into the garden.

Raíz

Earth-based cooking beside the waterfall.

Embér

Evenings around open flame under the canopy.

Experiences & community

Nothing is scheduled. The jungle sets the pace.

Into the Green

A near-silent walk through the Kaba-Kaba treeline, led at the pace of noticing.

First Breath

A guided breath set at Prána just after sunrise, before the day asks anything of you.

Contrast

Heat, cold, rest, repeat at ĀYLA. Sweat in the sauna, drop into the ice, then settle.

Float

Supported floating in Lago at last light, closer to sleep than most massages.

The Gold Hour

A slow close to the day as the light turns and the jungle quiets.
