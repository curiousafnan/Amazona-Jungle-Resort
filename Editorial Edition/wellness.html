Wellness

ĀYLA

Built low into basalt, made for recovery.

ĀYLA is the recovery engine of Amazona: a quiet space built into basalt and shadow, where heat, cold and stillness are used on purpose. Old methods, measured properly, and open through the day.

Contrast therapy

Cold plunge, then sauna, then rest. A simple protocol repeated in unhurried rounds.

Built into basalt

Low, dark and cool. The architecture holds temperature and quiet without effort.

Breath and stillness

Each round closes with slow breathing, so the body learns to come down as well as warm up.

Recovery

Recovery that runs all day, not once.

Recovery is part of daily life at Amazona, not a one-off treatment. Three simple tools, used in rounds.

1

Cold plunge

08° for ninety seconds. Cold exposure for circulation, focus and faster recovery.

2

Sauna

82° for twelve minutes. Heat to release tension and support repair after movement.

3

Rest

A quiet place to lie still between rounds, or the daybeds of Aluna Garden after.
