Pool & garden

Water and green, held over the jungle

Lago, an infinity pool at the level of the treetops, and Aluna Garden, a lounge planted into the green.

Infinity pool

Lago

A seamless edge, over the canopy. The water’s edge is a straight line the forest refuses to answer, precision on one side and wilderness on the other. Come down before seven, while the mist is still in the valley and the heat has not yet arrived.

Over the canopy

Held at the level of the treetops, so the swim happens inside the jungle.

Held to the light

Placed to catch the flat early light and the cool of the morning.

Quiet by design

No music and no bar noise. Birdsong and water over stone.

Garden lounge

Aluna Garden

Long afternoons, in the green. Aluna Garden is the slow middle of the day: deep daybeds and low sofas set among ferns and palms, open to the sky and the sound of the valley. Bring a book, a juice from Raíz, or nothing at all.

Daybeds in the shade

Deep cushions under the palms, for reading, napping or nothing.

Placed to the sun

Set along the arc of the sun, so light shapes the afternoon.

Juices from Raíz

Fresh juices and tonics brought down from the kitchen.

Left alone

No schedule and no interruptions. Rest is something you do here.
